## Educational generation and reference boundaries

Exam reasoning uses applicable assessment importance, weaknesses, prerequisites, uncertainty and deadline tradeoffs. Roadmap reasoning uses concrete outcomes and useful milestone detail. Mastery reasoning distinguishes demonstrated capability from time/completion and allows useful checkpoints/conditional remediation. General reasoning serves homework, catch-up, exploration and broad study without forcing a different policy. These policies share one generator and one validation boundary.

Array order expresses AI-declared educational importance; the allocator does not synthesize pedagogical scores. More capacity does not create work. Five-minute, one-activity and zero-new-work strategies are accepted when educationally justified. No duration floor beyond the frozen technical five-minute timebox, compulsory stage/check count, generic instruction replacement or lexical completion-criterion rule is introduced. Every supplied scope node must have an owned disposition; explicit exclusions survive. Credited scope needs owned scoped evidence flagged as demonstrating readiness. Completion, pace, confidence and saved self-reported check results cannot be promoted into that flag by model output.

New work is a strict bounded delta: at most 60 new activities across both selected targets. Owned kept phases/checkpoints are assembled before complete frozen blueprint validation, allowing a new contingency to reference a retained committed checkpoint without duplicating it. Final stored blueprints remain bounded to 200 activities, 500 scope nodes and 100 phases per Plan. New model identities cannot be UUIDs or execution aliases. Unknown scope/resource/evidence/activity references, changed policy, rewritten kept content and shared dependency cycles fail validation. Declared joint prerequisites have one Plan/activity owner; exact duplicate shared work is rejected. Semantic duplicate judgment still belongs to the later independent reviewer.

Only committed work is allocated. Optional and contingent activities remain unbooked; contingency requires a concrete condition at a committed checkpoint and never grants consent to apply a change. The generator chooses a useful checkpoint with 14-day detail normally or 28-day detail for suitable bounded work. Full strategic scope/phases remain in the blueprint. No deadline is fabricated.

## Capacity, scheduling and explicit feedback

The service loads Step 2 bounded model context and Phase 1 raw execution facts. Same-week actuals/commitments outside the detailed window are retained. Actual time is attributed to accepted session civil dates. Unselected open work remains committed. Selected started work reserves its owned remaining estimate in the envelope; selected kept unstarted work is reserved once by the allocator. Completed work preserves its approved content/placement and anchors new learning windows to its actual completion date, consuming no future capacity. Existing overload is surfaced rather than trimmed.

Daily and weekly ceilings are concurrent bounds. The soft recovery percentage is applied once at each bound; weekly recovery is capped by the disclosed percentage of the weekly ceiling and by recovery on remaining bookable days. Excluded dates reserve no weekly recovery. Per-goal ceilings further constrain the shared global envelope. One explicit adaptation can reduce the soft margin with a reason; it cannot change hard availability, rest, deadlines, commitments or ownership. Required work is not shortened to produce a green calendar.

Placement preserves hard prerequisites/windows, rest/floating/blocked dates, fixed deadlines and the exam-day rule, exact protected dates, accepted session maxima and all existing capacity facts. Stable topological search uses constrained date slack then declared order, accepted pacing and AI-declared cognitive demand. Search is bounded to 20,000 nodes by default; one deterministic local improvement pass preserves every hard constraint and pin.

Necessary upper bounds include indivisible activity fit, total constrained opportunity, weekly/global/per-goal limits, constrained deadline/date subsets and indivisible packing. `CAPACITY_SHORTFALL` requires such a proof under the disclosed detailed dates and margin. It does not assert that the entire goal or a later deadline is impossible. A failed bounded search returns `ALLOCATION_UNRESOLVED`. Every committed activity remains placed or explicitly unscheduled with its approved timebox. Findings expose binding constraints, spacing/advisory compromises, density/intensity, switching, booked finish implications, soft-target overruns and estimate risk. Soft findings invite educational judgment rather than automatic workload inflation.

Split expansion preserves each declared completion criterion and the original unsplit activity in a receipt. Every piece retains an original hard learning window; downstream dependencies and completion-based anchors target the final piece. Adjacent chunks below the technical minimum, identity collisions and expansion beyond the shared 60-activity envelope fail explicitly. Pieces remain marked `requiresReview`; expansion does not bypass Step 5 review.

## Durable adaptation and recovery

`LearningCompilerService.run(token, signal)` accepts only owned PLAN/REPLAN/JOINT Attempts. Advice and calendar-only operations cannot enter it. Accepted research must first be verified and adopted by Step 3. Unchanged intents bind through existing RevisionService; research-resolved intents keep their requested/resolved distinction and source receipts.

Every saved checkpoint pins request/root/authorization, target/base/intent/version/context correspondence, source hashes, envelope and educational/allocation digests. Owned allocation and forecast facts get a durable digest. Retained content is selected from the exact execution-spec seal, preventing a newer unapplied candidate from replacing active content. Consequential context changes, cancellation, expired leases and stale fences cannot persist late education. External model calls occur outside coordinated database transactions and use the Step 2 dispatcher, root budgets, deadlines and mandatory initial-review holdback (8,192 input/4,096 output tokens). Fallback calls are individually metered.

A consequential feasible schedule or infeasible allocation can invoke one adaptation. The service persists `ADAPTATION`, count one and `PENDING` before dispatch. The AI can keep the strategy or revise new education with named tradeoffs; either path re-allocates. Revised education is checkpointed before re-allocation. A takeover resumes saved blueprint/allocation work without repeating provider calls. If an adaptation was dispatched but its accepted response was not saved, recovery fails explicitly and requires a linked retry rather than repeating that adaptation. Unresolved final work preserves its artifact and moves the existing Attempt to `NEEDS_DECISION`.

A feasible result remains a RUNNING Attempt at ALLOCATION, with no Revision, candidate, quality pass, Quest write or apply. The Step 5 orchestrator must continue under the same lease/root and independently review the actual allocation. Production handler registration and `main.ts` readiness remain unchanged.

## Forecasts and supporting refinements

Forecasting uses 365 owned civil dates as a technical observation horizon, not a goal deadline or additional detailed segment. Per-Plan phase effort counts only work that Plan owns, excluding a shared prerequisite owned elsewhere. Booked remaining minutes are deducted once; aggregate estimates use the same daily/weekly/per-goal/rest/deadline rules. Missing effort produces `UNKNOWN_EFFORT`; an unknown earlier phase blocks a later overall progression forecast; estimates that do not finish in the observation horizon remain `BEYOND_FORECAST_WINDOW`. All are unbooked estimates, not mastery claims or promises of completion.

Two supporting refinements preserve the governing design and frozen contracts:

1. A completed protected activity can appear at its exact historical placement outside the detailed window. Open/new work remains within the detailed envelope. Reports preserve approved historical timeboxes separately from remaining capacity charges.
2. Retained phase estimates are historical inputs. If the generator does not explicitly reassess an unchanged phase definition, its estimate is marked unreviewed and forecast as unknown. This prevents old educational effort from being treated as fresh remaining work or elapsed time from being treated as mastery.

The complete owned-delta assembly, original split receipts, forecast/adaptation metadata, exact protected placement checks and hard-window DAG checks are additive supporting validation. Older checkpoints without planning metadata retain their previous shape. No architectural deviation was needed.
