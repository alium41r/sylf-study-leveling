# Blind reference plan (Claude Haiku, 13 November 2026)

A Haiku subagent was given only a student's message, with no Sylf context, no tools and no access to the repository or this work:

> Hi, I'm a student and I want to study for my midterms. Can you create me a plan for my Maths mid term: Complete Paper 1, CAIE A Levels? Today is Friday 13 November 2026 and my mid term is on Tuesday 1 December 2026. I struggle with planning what to study and when, so I want you to tell me exactly what I should study and when so I get the best grade I can.

Its answer, verbatim:

---

# Pure Maths 1 Midterm Plan (Fri 13 Nov to Tue 1 Dec)

You have 18 days. The plan covers all eight Paper 1 topics once, then spends the last few days on mixed practice and a full timed paper. Each day has a new-topic block and a practice block. I can't see your syllabus, notes or past papers, so check the topic list against what your teacher has taught, and adjust if your midterm covers less.

**Daily routine (weekdays about 1.5 to 2 hours, weekends about 3 hours):**
1. 10 minutes: redo 2 or 3 questions from the previous day without notes.
2. 40 to 60 minutes: learn the new topic. Watch or read the method, then write out the worked example yourself.
3. 40 to 60 minutes: practise past-paper questions on that topic (Paper 1 only). Mark them with the mark scheme.
4. 10 minutes: add every mistake to one error log, with the correct method written beside it.

## Rules for the plan

- **Do not skip the review days (20 Nov and 27 Nov).** Without them, early topics fade.
- **If you fall behind,** cut the Monday 23 Nov and Tuesday 24 Nov practice blocks in half before you cut any review.
- **If a topic is weak,** spend an extra 30 minutes on it on the next weekend day. Do not move on until you can do two past-paper questions on it without help.
- **Use real past papers.** Mark schemes show the method marks, which matter as much as the answers.

If you tell me which topics you find hardest, how many hours you can study on weekdays, and whether your midterm covers all eight topics, I can rebalance the schedule.

## Notes against the 9709 syllabus

The structure is sound and became the shape of the study-path compiler. Several items are not Paper 1 content: modulus functions, R-formulae, sec/cosec/cot, binomial expansion for rational n with its validity range, and the product and quotient rules belong to Paper 3 (or Paper 2). Volumes of revolution, which are Paper 1, are missing. This is why the planner uses a checked catalog where one exists and asks the model only for courses the catalog does not hold.
