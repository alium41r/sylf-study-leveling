## Freshness and recovery

- `PlannerCalendarCapacity.assertCurrent(ownerId, attemptId, capacityRef)` rereads canonical permissions, source content/versions, coverage and current execution. It does not rewrite history or authorize a call. Invoke it with the existing leased/provider admission at dependent boundaries; a check before a long call cannot guarantee freshness afterward.
- Snapshot validity is at most five minutes and can end at the next current-day minute boundary or earlier external expiry. Step 4 is conservative about elapsed time. Preserve a long strategy response against its original inputs if capacity changes while it reasons; do not shorten reasoning to meet snapshot freshness.
- Before education has been reserved/authored, `refresh(token, expectedCapacityRef, signal)` appends a new capacity/dossier pair with CAS ancestry and no provider call. It preserves original research, accepted intent, root/deadline/accounting and all prior versions. It refuses refresh once a non-cancelled strategy/translation/review reservation or artifact exists. Step 5 must deliberately implement the subsequent full revalidation/routing boundary before relaxing that safeguard; never change a saved response's input digest to the newest capacity.
- Learning/intent/source changes cannot use a Calendar-only refresh. Reuse the explicit research continuation and its accepted student decisions where needed, retaining original instructions, permissions, one root and finite absolute deadline. Unknown spending remains blocked until reconciliation.
- Calendar reads are cache-only. Missing/stale/offline coverage cannot become “no busy events.” Existing Calendar sync/settings own reconnection/refresh. No provider call or duplicate calendar store belongs in the capacity service.
