## Educational dimensions

Score each dimension separately, using the full accepted facts and proposed education. A useful alternative method is allowed. A fixed answer key illustrates factual checks; it is not an exact-output template.

| Score | Goal correspondence | Educational usefulness and proportion | Evidence and uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | Meets every consequential accepted requirement and offers a particularly clear achievable stopping gate | Necessary learning/checks are concrete, correct, efficient and well matched to the learner | Claims, resources, learner evidence and limitations are precise and well integrated |
| 4 | Useful without substantive correction; small wording edits may help | Actionable, correct and proportionate; no substantive extra/missing work | Honest and adequately grounded; no consequential unsupported assumption |

| 1 | Fundamentally fails or contradicts the task | Incorrect/impossible activity or no useful strategy | Fabricated consequential facts, evidence or availability |

Pass requires **all three scores ≥4 and no serious defect**, not an averaged score. Record a quoted accepted requirement, a quoted output segment and a concrete consequence for every failure. An aesthetic preference alone cannot lower a dimension below four. Blank/capped/incomplete output is UNASSESSABLE plus an operational failure, not a plausible strategy to finish on the model's behalf.

For prose-only reasoning, score an otherwise substantively complete STOP response verbatim despite a malformed simple wrapper, and separately mark envelope failure. Add no missing content. This reading-only assessment does not relax any artifact validator or qualify structured output.

## Serious versus minor

A serious defect materially defeats the accepted outcome, requires an unavailable authentic resource while claiming completion, teaches/requires an incorrect result, invents consequential provenance/assessment facts, violates protection/exclusion/authorization, or makes compulsory work substantially unnecessary/unachievable. A minor issue is wording/optional presentation that leaves the strategy useful without substantive change. Uncertainty is not proof of a violation; acknowledge it or use a conditional path where appropriate.

Effort is an estimate, not a stopwatch prediction. Assess whether actions contribute before the stopping gate and whether booked work agrees with the strategy. A larger timebox alone is not automatically a serious defect: examine the instructions, estimate range and actual contribution. Never impose a task-count/minute floor or require one preferred method if a valid alternative works.

## Stage-specific records

- **Reasoning:** three scores, serious/minor defects, envelope validity, stopping gate and resources. No requirement to supply a canonical schema.
- **Structure:** first-attempt STOP/usage/JSON, compact or reference schema, canonical ownership/reference/protection, full allocation outcome, three educational scores. Do not salvage invalid partial JSON. An allocator conflict is distinct from malformed transport.
- **Review:** expected defect/nondefect; raw identified issue; strict accepted finding; grounding validity; actual false/missed blocker and its consequence; appropriate preference/uncertainty basis if a concern is raised. For four defective cases, at least the consequential defect must be correctly identified with valid original requirement/proposal grounds. Extra false blockers fail precision even when a real defect is detected. Zero blockers on the eight nondefective cases is required. Empty findings need not invent advisory concerns.
- **Recovery (deferred):** each parseable rejected concern has one grounded correction/withdrawal; identity and evidence retained; no concern disappears; adjudicate withdrawal against original facts. An invalid objection does not become a blocker merely because it was returned first. Record primary failure even if recovery is excellent. No recovery is allowed in the active first block.
- **Repair:** literal full-object before/after diff; every original issue resolved; required dependencies/criteria also corrected; no unrelated educational edit; no protected change; canonical revalidation/allocation; original root usage and deadline; three scores on the complete result. An explanatory claim without a changed valid object is not correction.
- **Verification:** assessor judges actual resolution and all residual/new serious defects independently first. Compare every named RESOLVED/UNRESOLVED and new finding with that judgment. Any false resolution or missed serious residual/new issue fails. Correct rejection prevents publication but does not make the repair successful. Report sensitivity as NOT EXERCISED if no residual/new serious defect appears.
- **Capacity:** list commitments and contributions per sample. Match actions by educational meaning, not identity/title alone. Compare within each capacity and across capacities. A justified difference arising from feasibility may pass; capacity-only added curricula/repetition fails. Record minute ranges and variation without demanding exact equality.

## Blinding and adjudication

Assign neutral assessment IDs independently from execution IDs. Hide provider/config/interface labels and pair/order mapping until primary scores are locked. Show complete accepted facts and educational content; do not hide relevant workload or uncertainty to improve blinding. Format may remain apparent. Second assessor checks every severe failure, disputed blocker, withdrawal and claimed repair acceptance. Preserve both judgments and written resolution; do not silently average disagreements. If adjudication is pending, the gate is PENDING, not passed.

No labels or prompts may be edited in reaction to this batch. A discovered oracle error is recorded as INVALID_CASE, excludes that case from any success claim, and requires a later version/fresh confirmation rather than a favorable replacement in the same run.
