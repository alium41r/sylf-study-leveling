Updated 29 October 2026. Implements **Phase A and Phase B Steps 1–8**, technically qualified offline under the [approved handoff](AI_FIRST_PLANNER_HANDOFF.md) and [design rationale](reports/2026-10-25-ai-first-architecture-proposal/README.md). The complete journey retains trusted opt-in admission. Original Phase 3 adds an explicitly gated [student workspace](STUDENT_WORKSPACE.md) using the same services and execution boundary. ## Authority and responsibilities

Research establishes the accepted brief and material evidence, keeping verified retrieval, applicability, student reports, observed performance and uncertainty distinct. DeepSeek authors complete natural education. Translation expresses that education through existing compact assembly, without inventing required learning. The existing allocator owns placement. A fresh independent reviewer examines the complete dossier, strategy, translation, canonical education, execution and allocation, including all previous findings and previously missed/new defects.

The coordinator remains deterministic code in `server/compiler.ts`; no new manager model, service, queue, activity store or scheduler is introduced. No model response supplies permissions, durable identities, verified mastery, financial entitlement or execution authority. Advice remains read-only. Preparation and exact displayed-proposal confirmation remain separate actions.

## Strategy and faithful translation

A Strategy Document retains the exact final text, including whitespace, examples, conditions and Unicode, with immutable parent/version, producing call, prompt/input digests, effective model/fingerprint, effort, output cap and STOP finish. Hidden reasoning is never stored. The server creates paragraph anchors with **UTF-16 offsets into the exact retained text**, local to that version, and literal-span digests. Anchors are not assumed stable across text revisions. Annotation/rendering changes can remain cosmetic; any change to the retained educational text creates a new version and invalidates review.

The dossier pins exact accepted instruction, each requested/resolved intent and context, raw/adopted evidence, constraints, source versions/applicability, calendar projection, omissions and consequential questions. Full raw evidence remains in existing owned context/research/document services; the dossier is a versioned reference manifest, not another evidence database. Trusted research composition still has to establish semantic claim support; an excerpt hash does not establish educational truth.

A referenced capacity artifact retains the complete typed calendar projection and canonical SchedulingEnvelope, not just their hashes. Dossier validation checks both exact snapshots. This preserves the original placement inputs after calendar changes.

Translation receipts reference exact strategy versions and canonical blueprint digest. Their inventory maps consequential passages to owned activities/scope/phases/strategy/decisions, retained non-executing guidance, or explicit gaps. Meaning categories cover instruction, dependencies, evidence, criteria, conditions, stopping, uncertainty, estimates, resources, scope and guidance. Derivations are limited to server identity, exact arithmetic and declared split delivery. Every committed activity needs a strategy basis. Foreign references, missing anchors and changed canonical output fail. Conditions must remain contingent; useful long-term guidance is distinct from booked work.

Inventory completeness and fidelity are **semantic review duties**. A mapping table does not prove equivalence. Actual canonical reference/DAG/readiness/protection validators, split expansion and allocation remain mandatory; these foundation helpers are not an alternate execution compiler. Missing educational choices return to the strategist; missing personal facts return to Research. Shared prerequisites retain one owning Quest/effort charge, with correspondence expressed through the owning/dependent activities and exact canonical execution graph.

## Issues, checkpoints and refinement

Issue identities are server-owned UUIDs. Append dispositions `OPEN → ADDRESSED → VERIFIED_RESOLVED`, with explicit supported withdrawal from OPEN/ADDRESSED and reopening from ADDRESSED when verification fails. A resolved/withdrawn issue is immutable history; a newly found defect receives its own identity. Required evidence, offending content and severity remain stable. Changed diagnosis is a correction/withdrawal, not a silent rename or disappearance. Issue events bind the education digest; complete final review binds the issue heads as well. Literal grounds are checked against original registered strings, not JSON serialization or rejected claims.

Compiler-v2 checkpoints have research, strategy, translation, allocation, review, refinement, ready, incomplete and needs-decision stages. The transition contract permits substantive returns to the responsible role; it does not prescribe a maximum number of repairs. Persist pending call identity/input before dispatch and the accepted complete response/digest before deterministic assembly. Recovery distinguishes RESERVED, DISPATCHED uncertainty and RESPONSE_SAVED. A saved response resumes assembly without a provider call. An uncertain dispatch cannot be discarded/rebound or silently repeated. Checkpoint call references must correspond to existing durable accounting receipts. Takeover retains the archive, deadlines and usage.

Repeated candidate-plus-issue-set digests detect no-progress/cycles. The coordinator must investigate disputed objections and require credible benefit before continuing. Genuine operational, evidence, student-choice or progress boundaries preserve the best assessed draft/history with an incomplete/needs-decision result; they never create an approval. Review after semantic revision must check all named issues **and the complete result**, with full validation and actual reallocation.
