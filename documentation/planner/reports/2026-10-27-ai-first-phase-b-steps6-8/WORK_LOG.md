### Publication concurrency decision

Calendar-native/shared/cache changes must serialize with publication/confirmation, including phantom event insertions. Row reads alone cannot guarantee that. The implementation will extend existing Calendar/Planner transaction coordination with an account-scoped Calendar planning lock, acquired by relevant Calendar SQL mutation backstops and by final publication/apply. It will not introduce another Calendar store or scheduler. Ordinary research/capacity captures remain as-of reads; they do not take the final confirmation lock. Shared membership/owner changes and local external-calendar cache/catalog mutations require coverage too. Exact remote-provider changes remain subject to the existing cache-freshness limit and cannot be locked transactionally.

### Malformed-review recovery decision

Reuse existing rejected-concern capture and accountable correction/withdrawal. A known complete STOP response that deterministically fails review validation can produce a new explicitly linked logical review call, under the original resource root/deadline, with frozen complete facts and all rejected concerns. An uncertain dispatch, truncated/blank response or missing actual receipt cannot silently repeat. The actual full response remains in AiUsageLog. Repeated identical rejected work is a no-progress boundary, not an approval or a reason to weaken the rubric.

Decisions requiring changed product scope, providers/models, paid qualification, deployment or new execution semantics will be documented here with options and deferred while independent authorized work continues. None has been requested so far.
