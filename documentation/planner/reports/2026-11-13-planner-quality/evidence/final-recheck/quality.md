Topics:

1. **Sequences and series and their applications** w2 d2: Use arithmetic sequences and series and their sums; Use geometric sequences and series, including sums to infinity; Apply compound interest, depreciation and annuities; Solve problems with sigma notation and recurrence relations
2. **Exponents and logarithms** w2 d2: Apply the laws of exponents and logarithms; Solve exponential and logarithmic equations; Use logarithms to change bases; Model real situations with exponential functions; Work with logarithmic and exponential graphs and their asymptotes
3. **Proof and mathematical induction** w2 d3: Use direct proof and proof by counterexample; Use proof by contradiction, including irrationality of primes' roots; Apply the principle of mathematical induction to sums and divisibility; Use strong induction and induction with inequalities; Use the binomial theorem and Pascal's triangle
4. **Complex numbers** w3 d3: Perform arithmetic in Cartesian form and find conjugates and moduli; Convert between Cartesian, polar and Euler forms; Multiply and divide complex numbers in polar form and use de Moivre's theorem; Find complex roots of equations and represent them in the Argand diagram; Solve quadratic and polynomial equations with complex roots
5. **Counting principles and partial fractions** w2 d2: Use permutations, combinations and the multiplication principle; Expand binomial expressions with negative or fractional indices and find general terms; Decompose rational expressions into partial fractions with distinct or repeated linear factors; Solve systems of linear equations by elimination and with matrices or row reduction
6. **Polynomial functions** w2 d2: Use the factor and remainder theorems; Find sums and products of roots of polynomials; Solve polynomial equations and inequalities; Sketch polynomials showing intercepts, multiplicity and end behaviour
7. **Functions, graphs and transformations** w3 d2: Identify domain, range, composite and inverse functions; Recognise odd, even and self-inverse functions; Sketch and analyse rational functions including asymptotes and holes; Apply translations, stretches and reflections to graphs; Solve equations and inequalities involving modulus functions
8. **Geometry and trigonometry** w3 d3: Use radians, arc length and sector area; Use the Pythagorean, reciprocal and quotient identities; Apply compound angle and double angle identities; Solve trigonometric equations over a given interval; Sketch trigonometric graphs and use their symmetry and periodicity; Work with inverse trigonometric functions and their domains
9. **Vectors** w3 d3: Add, subtract and scale vectors and find magnitudes; Use the scalar product to find angles and test perpendicularity; Find vector equations of lines and their intersections; Find equations of planes and intersections of lines and planes; Solve problems with vectors in two and three dimensions
10. **Statistics and probability** w3 d2: Calculate and interpret measures of centre, spread and correlation; Use linear regression lines to make predictions; Calculate probabilities with Venn diagrams, tree diagrams and conditional probability; Apply Bayes' theorem and independence; Use discrete and continuous random variables with expectation and variance; Work with the binomial and normal distributions, including standardisation
11. **Probability distributions, probability density** w2 d2: Use discrete probability distributions including the binomial distribution; Standardise variables and use the normal distribution in problems; Work with continuous probability density functions, including median; Apply expectation algebra: E(aX+b), Var(aX+b) and E(X+Y); Solve problems using these distributions in context
12. **Limits, continuity and differentiability** w3 d3: Understand limits, continuity and differentiability, including L'Hôpital's rule; Differentiate from first principles and use standard derivatives; Apply the chain, product and quotient rules; Differentiate implicit functions and inverse and reciprocal trigonometric functions; Find equations of tangents and normals and analyse graph behaviour; Solve optimisation problems and kinematics problems
13. **Integration and differential equations** w3 d3: Integrate standard functions and use definite integrals for area; Integrate by substitution and by parts, including partial fractions; Find volumes of revolution; Solve first-order differential equations by separating variables; Solve first-order linear differential equations with an integrating factor; Use Euler's method to approximate solutions
14. **Maclaurin series** w2 d3: Find Maclaurin series for standard functions; Use series to approximate values and limits; Combine and differentiate known series; Use convergence tests such as the ratio test
15. **Applications of calculus** w2 d2: Find equations of tangents and normals to curves; Solve optimisation problems using differentiation; Use kinematics with displacement, velocity, acceleration and total distance travelled; Distinguish convergence and divergence of sequences and series; Study the behaviour of functions graphically using calculus
